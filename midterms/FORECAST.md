## Model details

**Model A — midterm referendum (n = 20 midterms, 1946–2022).**
President's-party seat change regressed on approval and seats held:

```
seat_change = -24.6 +1.08 x (approval - 50) -0.08 x (seats_held - 218)
```

Residual SE 18.4 seats; leave-one-out RMSE 19.4 seats (used as the simulation scale). Point estimate for 2026: -37 seats for the president's party.

**Model B — generic ballot → seats (n = 6 midterms, 2002–2022).**
Final-poll error: mean -1.6, SD 3.4 pts, plus drift for time remaining. Seats-votes curve: `dem_seats = 212.5 + 2.72 x dem_margin` (residual SE 6.4 seats).

## Caveats

- Fundamentals only — no district-level data, candidate quality,
  redistricting/mid-decade map changes, or incumbency effects.
- Tiny samples (20 and 6 elections). Uncertainty is wide on purpose;
  t-distributed errors give fat tails.
- The seats-votes curve is fit on post-2000 maps and shifts whenever
  maps are redrawn.
- Update `inputs_2026.json` (approval, generic ballot) before relying
  on any number here.
